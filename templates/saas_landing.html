Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my existing inventory from Excel?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet — the platform maps common column names automatically and imports your products in seconds.

Does it work for my type of store?

Yes. The platform adapts to your industry — clothing boutiques see "Size" and "Materials Quality" fields; appliance stores see "Model" and "Condition"; toy stores, art galleries, mini-markets and more are supported.

Can I show multiple photos per product?

Yes. Upload up to 8 photos per item — front, back, detail shots, variations. Thumbnails are auto-generated for fast loading.

What happens when a customer reserves a product?

The reservation is saved with the customer's name, phone, and email. You see it in your dashboard and can follow up to confirm pickup.

How do I try the platform before buying?

Click "Try The Live Demo" at the top of this page. You'll enter a read-only demonstration with sample data. No signup, no credit card.

Already a customer? How do I log in?

Click "Store Owner Login" in the top navigation. Select your store and enter your password.
