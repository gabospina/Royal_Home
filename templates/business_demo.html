Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my existing Excel inventory?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet with your products and the platform will import them — including names, prices, and categories.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The platform is fully responsive. Your customers can browse and reserve products from any device, and you can manage inventory from your phone.

Can I run more than one store location?

Yes. The platform supports single-location and multi-location retailers from one dashboard.

What happens when a customer reserves a product?

The reservation is saved with the customer's name, phone, and email. You'll see it in your dashboard and can follow up to confirm pickup.

How do I try the platform before buying?

Click "Try The Live Demo" anywhere on this page to log into a read-only demo with sample data. No signup required.

Is my data safe?

Your inventory and customer data stay in your account. The demo uses sample data only and cannot access your real records.
